## Fishing

- **Rare fish.** Each catch can be common, rare or golden. Each one sells for a different price.
  - Common: most catches. Sells for the normal price.
  - Rare: caught less often. Sells for more.
  - Golden: very rare. Sells for much more.
  - Open questions: how often each kind is caught, and the exact prices.
- **Lucky golden zone.** Sometimes a gold zone shows up for 10 seconds and pays double.
- **Night fish.** At night, different fish bite.
- **Market prices.** The sell price goes up and down. You choose when to sell.
- **Buyer requests.** A buyer asks for one fish type and pays a bonus.

## Company

- **Helper boats.** You buy a boat that fishes by itself.
  - Fishes by itself inside a zone.
  - Fills its own hold. You collect the fish and sell them.
  - Open questions: what does it cost, how many boats can you have, and does it fish while you are away?

## Look and feel

- **Tape tabs and tilt.** A small tape tab on each card, and a slight tilt, so the cards feel like real paper.
- **Price tag slap-down.** The contract banner slaps down like a price tag.

## Sound and saving

- **Sound.** Splash, coin, and a happy tune when you finish a contract. Not decided.
