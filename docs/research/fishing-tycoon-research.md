# Fishing Tycoon - Research

Facts here have a link. Ideas without a link are in `docs/made-up-facts/fishing-tycoon-ideas.md`.

## Game summary

Fishing Tycoon is a Three.js browser game (keyboard only). You sail a boat to moving fishing zones, fish, sell the catch, and buy upgrades. Three contracts set a fish quota. Source: the project files PRODUCT.md and PLAN.md (local, no web link).

## What they did well

- Clear loop: catch, sell, upgrade (DREDGE, Idle Fishing, Gone Fishin'). Links in the table above.
- Upgrades open new places and rarer fish (Cat Goes Fishing: https://vaporlens.app/app/343780/cat_goes_fishing.md).
- Cat Goes Fishing has a warning: a limited fish supply can block the player from getting fish for upgrades (same link). Reviews are mixed.
